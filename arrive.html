The first week

Arriving and settling in

Arrival day is the most information you will ever get at once: luggage, a room, paperwork, and buildings you cannot find. This page puts it in an order you can just follow.

Important: the documents you need and the registration process can change from year to year. The offer letter and the university's own notices are what count. This page is about the order and the experience, not the official rules.

Do these in this order

  1. Register first, and collect your room slip

    Bring originals and photocopies of everything. You need the room slip before you go looking for the building.

  2. Drop your luggage at the room and check your bed

    Do this before anything else. Dragging a suitcase around while you queue for paperwork is miserable.

  3. Sort out your campus card, door access and internet

    These are usually handled in the same place. Ask what the opening hours are before you walk over.

  4. Buy what you need for the first night

    See the checklist below. Do not try to buy everything at once.

Bank card / payment receiptAs instructed by the university

What you need on the first night

  • Bedding — a mosquito net is genuinely useful here
  • Power strip and an adapter if you need one
  • Toiletries and slippers
  • Basic medicine: insect repellent, anti-itch cream, something for your stomach
  • An umbrella — the rain here arrives fast

Easy things to forget

  • Do not bring only one photocopy of anything. The print shop is busy on registration day.
  • Save your tutor's phone number in your phone, not just in a group chat.
  • Photograph the electricity and water meter readings when you move in.

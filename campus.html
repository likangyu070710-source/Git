Once you have moved in

Campus and nearby

Most days my walk is short: down from the dorm, across to the first floor of the Student Center for a meal, then over to the teaching building. The parcel lockers are in my own block, so even the errands do not take me far. Use the buttons to narrow the list.

Everyday places

Showing all places

Student canteen

Where: first floor of the Student Center

What: the everyday canteen — plain, filling meals at the lowest price on campus

Advice: this is where you will eat most days. It fills up the moment classes end, so go early or accept the queue.

Flavour restaurant

Where: the other half of the same floor

What: the floor is split in two. This half is the 风味餐厅 — food from other regions instead of the plain everyday menu.

Advice: come to this side when the ordinary canteen starts to taste the same every day. Same floor, so look at both before you queue.

On-campus shop

Good for: water, instant noodles, tissues, laundry detergent — the urgent things

Advice: usually a little more expensive than off campus. Buy in bulk in town or online.

Parcel lockers

Where: in the dorm buildings, on the ground floor of your own block

How: the courier sends a pickup code to your phone. Type the code into the locker, the door opens, take your parcel. No desk, no queue.

Advice: collect it the day it arrives. A pickup code does not stay valid forever, and a locker is not a cupboard.

Print shop

Good for: printing handouts, photocopying documents, binding

Advice: it is busiest during registration week. Print your photocopies before you travel if you can.

Getting into town

How: a bus from the gate, or a taxi into the city

Advice: find out which gate the bus leaves from before the day you need it, and check the first and last times once. Services thin out after dark.

Street food just outside the gate

Where: walking distance from the main gate

Advice: busiest after evening classes. Pick the stalls with the most people at them.

Small things that save time

  • Save your timetable as the first photo in your camera roll. Faster than opening an app every time.
  • Drop pins for your dorm, the teaching building and the canteen on a map in your first week. You will stop getting lost.
  • Keep an umbrella with you — the rain here arrives faster than any forecast.

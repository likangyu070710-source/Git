Once you have moved in

Campus and nearby

After the first week you only really use a handful of places. Here they are, grouped by what you need them for. Use the buttons to filter.

Everyday places

Showing all places

Canteen 1

Where: about 5 minutes on foot from the dorms

When to go: avoid 12:00–12:30, that is the rush

Advice: the second floor is cheaper; the first floor costs a bit more but you get served faster.

Canteen 2 / halal counter

Where: on the teaching-building side

Advice: come here when you only have a 40-minute break between classes — it is much closer than walking back to the dorms.

On-campus shop

Good for: water, instant noodles, tissues, laundry detergent — the urgent things

Advice: usually a little more expensive than off campus. Buy in bulk in town or online.

Parcel collection point

Bring: your collection code on your phone; some desks also ask for your student card

Advice: the queue is long at the start of term. Go outside the early-evening rush. For anything large, ask first whether they will hold it.

Print shop

Good for: printing handouts, photocopying documents, binding

Advice: it is busiest during registration week. Print your photocopies before you travel if you can.

Getting into town

How: bus from the gate, or a taxi — write down the exact pickup point

Advice: check the first and last bus times once, and remember that services thin out after dark.

Street food just outside the gate

Where: walking distance from the main gate

Advice: busiest after evening classes. Pick the stalls with the most people at them.

Small things that save time

  • Save your timetable as the first photo in your camera roll. Faster than opening an app every time.
  • Drop pins for your dorm, the teaching building and the canteen on a map in your first week. You will stop getting lost.
  • Keep an umbrella with you — the rain here arrives faster than any forecast.

Written by a current BiUH student · 2026
